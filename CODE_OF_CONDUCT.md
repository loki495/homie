# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We ask that you respect others,
their perspectives, and their lived experiences. No harassment, discrimination, or disrespect will be tolerated.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Listen and engage genuinely with different perspectives
- Focus on the work and ideas, not personal criticism
- Assume good intent while addressing impact
- Welcome feedback and be open to different approaches

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Disrespectful or hostile language
- Deliberate exclusion or undermining of others
- Any form of abuse

## Enforcement

Issues can be reported by opening a private vulnerability report or by contacting the maintainer directly.
All reports will be taken seriously and handled with discretion.
